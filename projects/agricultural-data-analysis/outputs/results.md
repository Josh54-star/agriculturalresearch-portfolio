# Analytical Results

## Kenya maize yield, 1960–2010

The dataset contains 51 annual observations. Mean maize yield was **1,528.7 kg/ha**, with a minimum of 1,071.3 kg/ha and maximum of 2,071.2 kg/ha.

### Historical pattern

Average yield increased from approximately **1,200 kg/ha in the 1960s** to **1,775 kg/ha in the 1980s**, before declining to approximately 1,635 kg/ha in the 2000s.

### Trend model

An OLS model of maize yield on calendar year estimates an average historical increase of **10.26 kg/ha per year** (robust SE = 1.82; p < 0.001). The model explains approximately **32.8%** of the variation in annual yield.

### Interpretation

The series shows a positive long-run trend, but the pattern is not monotonic. The rise through the 1980s is followed by substantial year-to-year variation and lower average yields in the 1990s and 2000s. The time-trend coefficient should be interpreted as a descriptive historical association rather than a causal estimate.

### Reproducibility note

All reported values are stored in the accompanying CSV tables and generated by the R workflow in `R/`. The dataset is a public-source snapshot; no confidential farmer-level data are used.
