# Climate-Smart Agriculture, Poultry Productivity & Household Income

## Research portfolio project

### Overview

This project investigates the relationship between adoption of climate-smart agricultural practices and poultry productivity and household income among smallholder farmers in Bungoma County, Kenya.

The project brings together my interests in agricultural production, climate resilience, farmer decision-making, household livelihoods, and applied quantitative research.

### Research problem

Smallholder poultry systems are exposed to climate-related pressures, including heat stress, disease risks, feed and water constraints, and changing production conditions. Climate-smart practices may help farmers improve resilience while maintaining or increasing productivity, but the benefits of adoption need to be evaluated empirically.

### Main research question

How does adoption of climate-smart agricultural practices relate to poultry productivity and household income among smallholder farmers?

### Key analytical dimensions

- Climate-smart practice adoption
- Poultry productivity
- Household agricultural income
- Farmer and household characteristics
- Farm management and production conditions
- Access to agricultural extension and information

### Proposed analytical workflow

1. Clean and validate survey data
2. Construct indicators of climate-smart practice adoption
3. Describe adoption patterns and farmer characteristics
4. Estimate associations between adoption and poultry productivity
5. Examine relationships with household income
6. Conduct robustness and diagnostic checks
7. Interpret results in relation to climate resilience and agricultural policy

### Methods and tools

- R
- Stata
- Python where useful for data preparation and visualization
- Descriptive statistics
- Regression modelling
- Econometric diagnostics
- Data visualization

### Reproducibility

The final repository will separate raw data, processed data, scripts, outputs, and documentation. Sensitive farmer-level information will not be published.

### Status

**Research project / portfolio case study — development stage.**

Empirical findings will only be reported after analysis of the underlying dataset. This repository currently documents the research question and analytical framework rather than presenting unverified results.
